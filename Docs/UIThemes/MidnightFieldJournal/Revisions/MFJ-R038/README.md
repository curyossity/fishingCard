A newly generated2196×716 transparent parchment strip replaces the metallic housing in the new Gameplay E comparison only. Fine brown ink rules, tiny ochre diamonds, paper grain and restrained navy marine sketches match the cards. The760×175 display footprint, label/value/status fields and per-capacity segments remain independent. Normal is navy ink, full is sepia, overload is oxide red with explicit excess text. Graphical fill saturates; actual overloaded numerals remain visible. Three source states show4/8,8/8 and10/8 (+2), plus the normal assembled copy. No new mechanics or break-chance thresholds are introduced.

The border and botanical detail stay at the margins to leave a quiet writing area. The label/values use Cinzel and Source Serif4; state segments are editable native Penpot rectangles. Source aspect and alpha margins are preserved. No slicing, source-art editing or font installation was needed. Artwork was generated with built-in imagegen using the Technique frame only as a style reference.

The previously imported MFJ-R035 meter remains in Unity and the older Penpot assemblies. Its completed status is retained; this new proposal remains pending review and any later requested integration. A future import should replace the independent housing, ink colors, field geometry and segment styling in LineLoadMeterView/JournalLoadSegment and their builders, preserving runtime effective-load/capacity ownership and the separate haul display. Review PNGs must not be used as runtime gauge images.
