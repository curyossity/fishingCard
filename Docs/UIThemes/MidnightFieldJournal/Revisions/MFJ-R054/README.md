# MFJ-R054 — Ornate Tackle Shop

One expanded Tackle Shop screen continuing the decorative direction of [MFJ-R053 Harbor](../MFJ-R053/README.md). The user requested the shop with the same instructions: match the creature cards' detailed naturalist engraving rather than the simpler R052 shop.

## Penpot

Page: **Concept B — Reusable Assembly (4K)**. Board: **MFJ-R054 — Tackle Shop / Ornate card-style study**, at `(52700,31600)`, size `3840 × 2160`. Exact shape and field IDs are recorded in [PenpotDesign.json](PenpotDesign.json).

Layered parchment and copper frames, textured navy information areas, engraved kelp and rocks, warm workshop lighting and matching typography continue the revised Harbor. Independent equipment illustrations and native text sit above the newly generated blank workshop composition. The screen distinguishes equipped, purchasable and journal-locked items, shows the single equipment slot and keeps gold as the only currency. Back to Harbor navigates to R053.

Names, prices and ownership states are illustrative design data inherited from the earlier study, not implemented or approved equipment definitions. No new equipment effects or progression rules are introduced. The selected item panel communicates permanent unlock and the one-equipped-item choice without inventing detailed mechanics.

## Approval and production boundary

This is a visual review composition. Equipment artwork and text are independent; scenery and decorative shells remain one static newly generated layer. Do not slice the composition into production assets. After approval, independently recreate the background and decorative shells at production resolution, retaining separate native labels, ownership states, input regions and equipment artwork. A 3840 × 2160 Penpot export is a layout review, not evidence that the source raster is native 4K.

No Unity code, assets, prefabs or scenes are changed or imported. R053 remains pending and is not superseded. The earlier R052 shop remains preserved as historical comparison.
