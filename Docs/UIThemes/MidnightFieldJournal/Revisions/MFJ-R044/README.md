# MFJ-R044 — Whole portraits and no scrollbar

**Superseded by MFJ-R045, imported at MFJ-SYNC-007. Historical source record; do not import this draft.**

Penpot only; ready for review, not integrated into Unity. Refines R043 instances in place on **Concept B — Reusable Assembly (4K)**. Select **MFJ-R043 / Compact modifier tooltips / 1–3 sources** or its adjacent overflow board. Board names and IDs remain stable for this correction.

Removed scrollbar tracks and thumbs from both overflow examples and both prototype states. The three-reason viewport, scroll-state navigation and “more below” / “above” indicators remain. Future Unity scrolling must work without a visible scrollbar.

All seven creature portraits across the examples and prototypes now preserve their aspect ratio, fit fully inside a centered 48×48 area and retain at least four pixels of padding in the 56×56 icon tile. They no longer fill by height and clip their sides. Technique/event icons and the reusable nine-region frame are unchanged.

R044 replaces the pending R043 design contract. R042 remains a comparison alternative; choose R042 or R044 before integration. All completed Unity integrations and MFJ-SYNC-006.16 remain unchanged.
