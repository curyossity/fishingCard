# Background creation and provenance

The built-in imagegen tool generated an original background concept from the prompt below. Actual output was 1672 × 941, so it was retained as **reference-only** in `Art/GeneratedConceptOnly.png`. It is not placed in Penpot and is not enlarged for the game.

The final background combines new vector paper and edge geometry at **3840 × 2160** in `Art/ShopBackground4K.svg` with the independent new transparent engraving `Art/CornerOrnament.png`. The engraving is 1536 × 1024, displayed at 210 × 140 or smaller; two page-corner instances and one navy-margin instance are authored in Penpot. The SVG is the base layer, not a flattened copy of the whole composition. Penpot exports the assembled `Art/ShopBackground4K.png` directly at 3840 × 2160. No old decorative image, screenshot slices, cropped corner art or raster upscaling are used.

## Image-generation concept prompt

Create a BRAND NEW standalone background artwork for a 4K PC nautical fishing card-game Tackle Shop UI. Exact output resolution 3840 x 2160, landscape 16:9. This is a new background, not an edit, screenshot, UI mockup or enlargement. Render all engraving at native full-canvas resolution with extremely crisp fine linework, no blur, no depth of field, no shallow focus. Entire image opaque. Composition must leave the existing UI overlay undisturbed: dark ink navy (#101D24) textured paper across the full canvas; one large warm pale ivory parchment inventory leaf occupying x=70..2510, y=65..2095. The remaining right third stays calm dark navy for a separate large equipment card placed later. No shop room, scenery, shelf or panorama. No cards, item illustrations, panels, slots, words, lettering, titles, numbers, coins, buttons or UI elements baked into the image. The page should feel like a rare hand-engraved fisherman's equipment folio in a detailed naturalist card game: warm worn parchment, sepia/copper ink contours, deep navy stippling, tiny ochre accents, refined tactile drypoint hatching, fine hand-drawn coastal botanical flourishes. Make UNIQUE NEW decorations designed specifically for this shop background: delicate rigging-line curls with little tied loops along the extreme page edges, small coastal seed pods and narrow sea-grass sprigs interwoven with miniature hand-engraved float and line-spool motifs in the LOWER LEFT and LOWER RIGHT page margin only. Distinct from leafy rock-corner card frames: no rock piles, coral banks, medallions, diamond corners, ornate metal or shiny hardware. Do not reuse an existing frame style. Thin organic double ink page edging, graceful broken-line flourishes, lower corners more developed, top corners restrained and tiny. Decorations must remain within the outermost 90 pixels of the parchment leaf edges, except lower left motif which may extend to x=270,y=1980..2080 and lower right motif x=2320..2480,y=1980..2080. Keep all of x=180..2340,y=180..1980 quiet and nearly blank with subtle paper grain only: the game will overlay equipment slots and catalog there. Top-left navigation area x=190..650,y=100..170 must stay blank. On the navy right third, add just a whisper of fine copper/ink rigging-line and sea-grass at the extreme bottom-right edge, within x=3720..3839; no large shapes behind the detail card. The background should be richly crafted at the margins but calm and light behind text. Sharp publication-quality engraved detail at native 3840x2160. No artificial glow, no bevelled gold frame, no Photoshop-looking gradients. Return only this new background artwork.
