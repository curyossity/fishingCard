## Final prompt

Create ONE new standalone blank EQUIPMENT CARD UI FRAME sprite for a richly engraved nautical fishing card game. Reference images supplied are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: match their hand-engraved copper/ochre ink, navy blue botanical linework, warm ivory parchment grain, intricate tactile illustrated borders. DO NOT copy their layout or marine landscape. This new equipment family must be recognizably distinct from creature, event and technique cards. Exact portrait canvas ratio 1190:1322 (width/height 0.90015), frame fills canvas with just 2 percent transparent margin, straight-on flat 2D game asset, no perspective or shadow outside. Produce at least 1536 pixels wide and proportionate height for use at 4K. Design identity: fine braided fishing-line and tied-knot corner ornaments, elegant curving hook-shaped copper ink filigree and small dry coastal fronds confined to narrow margins. No shiny metal or mechanical gears. Rounded rectangular corners, multiple very fine engraved keylines, dense delicate stippling at corners. Three blank regions: a warm ivory top title zone from y=.04 to .20 (small category line and large name space, WITHOUT ANY TEXT); large calm pale parchment illustration field from y=.20 to .70, very subtle fishing-line curls near the sides only, no illustrated scene, no fish, no object, no rock bank, no underwater landscape; lower single deep navy effect/status panel from y=.73 to .95 with flat quiet center for future native text. A very slim ivory separator at y=.70-.73. A small line-knot ink motif embedded at separator center, not a round rarity gem or medallion. Keep the lower panel sides decorated with restrained rope/thread flourishes and tiny fronds, leaving central 70 percent clean for text. No weight, value, numbers, icons of money, rarity hooks, sockets, Catch/Bait panels, words, labels, lettering, demonstration item or mockup background. All type and object artwork will be separate editable layers added later. Asset is independent new artwork, not a slice from any screenshot. transparent exterior.
