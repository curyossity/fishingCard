# Separate Landing panel artwork

Active presentation revision, 25 November 2026. [Watch the updated combo and compare the edges](Compare.html).

The previous moving faces sampled rectangular regions of the complete Landing machine. Those regions included cabinet pixels outside the panels' beveled edges, so movement, deformation and close-ups exposed square cutout corners and stray trim. Both panels now use independent `LandingPanelArtwork` assets under `Assets/UIThemes/TackleApparatus/Art/Landing/Separated/`.

Each asset contains a native source-sprite slice and an editable eight-point convex silhouette. `LandingCollisionGraphic` draws only that silhouette, leaving the surrounding pixels transparent through omitted geometry. The two sprites share the unchanged master texture; this adds no duplicate texture memory, generated painting, resampling or replacement artwork. Their own brass/ivory bevels remain part of the panels. [Artwork.json](Artwork.json) records source hash, dimensions, provenance and registration.

Both retain their 88% scale, centers at (873, 422) and (1393, 422), and original native-field offsets. The fixed machine and continuous recess remain stationary behind them. The canonical dash, squash/stretch, recoil, turns, water and accepted sound assets are preserved. Earlier and ordinary hits keep normal speed and framing. `LandingPanelArtworkSetup` creates missing assets while preserving existing authored contours, source slices and GUIDs on repeated authoring.
