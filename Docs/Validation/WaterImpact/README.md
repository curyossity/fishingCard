# Custom water collision and score punch

Active presentation revision, 24 November 2026. [Watch modest and powerful native examples](Compare.html), or inspect the [consecutive motion frames](Motion.png).

This increment adds an original PixelFX water effect at the physical seam where the machine creature card meets the score card. A short white foam core expands into rounded curved water jets, then shaded droplets and small hollow bubbles. The effect uses three authored strength sheets, selected by the existing contribution-gain strength. A larger operation produces a larger, fuller splash. Only the native score digits punch in scale (roughly 5-14%), settling within 160 ms; the receiver frame and other labels retain their existing shape.

The existing collision, recoil, shake, 210 ms three-turn encounter boundaries and single contributor flips are preserved. Point delivery and existing sounds retain their exact timestamps. No scoring rule, player state, supplied artwork or audio asset changes. No new projectile is introduced. Reduced motion suppresses both spray and number scaling. Turns, new preparation, misses, skip, replacement, component disable and phase exit clear the effect, preventing an earlier spray from returning after a flip.

`LandingWaterImpact` owns its non-intercepting native RawImage atlas playback and score-number scale. It samples the same unscaled sequence clock as `LandingCardCollision`, which exposes the moving contact seam. `LandingWaterImpactSetup`, retained by normal Landing authoring, reads the exported PixelFX frame/grid/pivot metadata and binds three point-filtered, uncompressed RGBA atlases. No per-hit GameObjects, Sprite instances, physics particles or gameplay randomness are created. Runtime ownership and cleanup stay in the existing score presentation.

The maintained [recipe and rendering instructions](../../../Tools/PixelFX/README.md) remain under `Tools/PixelFX` in the repository; installed PixelFX stays under ignored `.local-tools`.
