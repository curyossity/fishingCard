# Earshot percussion source credits

Reconstruction engine pinned to 7a7f7c5c70d4a763a1eca4b3666af7c77e313f97. These are the credited CC0 recording families behind the selected local models; the game uses rendered model outputs, not these recordings.

Every model in this folder is a few kilobytes of numbers (earshot.synth.resyn) analysed from one recording in the public domain (CC0 1.0); no audio is shipped. Each design plays its recordings as its variations (`kick/punch` plays the recordings of `kick/soft` at a harder velocity). The recordings and their authors:
